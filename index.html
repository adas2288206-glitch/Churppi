the house

A first-floor kitchen built around the momo basket

Churppi sits one floor up in Lakshmiganj Bazar, just off GT Road — easy to miss from the street, easy to love once you're seated. The room leans into its Himalayan roots without overdoing it: warm wood, low light, and the quiet clatter of steamer baskets from the kitchen.

The kitchen keeps its list tight — momos in every style, a short run of Bhutanese-style datshi, thukpa, wok specials and pork dishes — so everything that comes out is made to order, not reheated. Diners keep coming back for the same two things: how gentle the staff are, and how far ₹200–400 stretches here.
